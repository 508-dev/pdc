There is no total row, and there will not be one. Directing the phosphorus to grain gives the valley more food energy overall and leaves Chakar with nothing: it has no grain farm, so its only food arrives through alfalfa and the dairy herd. A single valley-wide figure would report the aggregate as an improvement and hide the community that got zero. Preserving that is the point of never reducing across dimensions or across communities.
